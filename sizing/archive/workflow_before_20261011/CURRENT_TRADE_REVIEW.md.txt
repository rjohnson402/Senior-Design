## Interpretation and limits

AR18 remains the requested ceiling. A selected AR18 point is a bounded result, not evidence of a global structural optimum. Likewise, a taper or diameter endpoint identifies work still limited by the search/model.

Landing reverse-thrust credit is 0% of weight (zero in the revised baseline). The separate clean-stall enforcement flag is 0. Motor ratings remain disk-area proportional. No RPM/torque/ground-clearance, detailed aeroelastic, post-failure energy-access or wingtip-swirl model is added. Very small mathematical prop passes can demand unrealistic installed ratings; use the minimum-MTOW selections.

The diameter bounds are engineering search choices, not validated hardware limits. For scale, NASA described 5-ft cruise and 1.9-ft high-lift props for X-57; its twelve-high-lift-prop architecture differs from this six-prop concept. [NASA X-57 overview](https://www.nasa.gov/centers-and-facilities/armstrong/x-57-maxwell/).
